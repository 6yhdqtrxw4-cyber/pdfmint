FAQ

Are my files really not uploaded?

Correct. All processing happens inside your browser using a local PDF engine (pdf-lib). You can verify by turning off your internet after the page loads — everything still works.

Is it really free?

Yes. Merge and split are free with no watermarks. Future tools may include an optional Pro tier (batch processing, compression), but the core stays free.

Is there a file size or page limit?

No hard limit — it depends on your device's memory. For typical documents (up to a few hundred pages) it works smoothly.

Can I rearrange pages while merging?

Yes — after adding files you can reorder them, and when splitting you can type any page range like 1-3, 5, 8-12.
