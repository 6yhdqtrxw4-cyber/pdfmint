Why merge PDFs on-device?

Most online PDF mergers upload your documents to a remote server, process them, and send them back. That means contracts, invoices, medical records, or unpublished work pass through someone else's computer — often in a country with different privacy laws.

PDFMint's merge tool runs a complete PDF engine (pdf-lib) inside your browser:

  • No upload — your files stay on your device, verifiable by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads
  • No file size limit — limited only by your device's memory, not a free-tier quota
  • No watermark, no sign-up — what you merge is exactly what you download
  • Reorder before merging — drag files in, rearrange with ↑ ↓, then combine

How to merge PDF files

  • Click "Merge PDF" and drop your files (or click to browse)
  • Use the arrows to arrange them in the right order
  • Press "Process → Download" and get a single merged PDF

FAQ

Is merging PDFs here really free?

Yes — free, unlimited, no watermark. The processing cost is yours (your own CPU), so there is nothing for us to meter.

Can I merge PDFs offline?

Yes. After the page finishes loading once, you can turn off your internet connection and merge as many files as you like.

Will the quality or links survive merging?

Pages are copied losslessly from the original files. Internal quality does not degrade; most links and bookmarks are preserved by the underlying engine.

How many files can I merge at once?

There is no hard cap. Practical limits depend on your device's memory — dozens of large files work fine on modern laptops and phones.
