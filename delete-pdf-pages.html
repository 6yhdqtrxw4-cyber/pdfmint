Why delete pages on-device?

The pages you want to remove are usually exactly the ones you don't want anyone else to see — salary slips in a bank statement, an internal appendix, a page with a signature. Most online tools ask you to upload the whole file first. PDFMint's delete tool runs a complete PDF engine (pdf-lib) inside your browser:

  • No upload — the document never leaves your device; disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works
  • Lossless for the pages you keep — kept pages are copied byte-for-byte: text stays selectable, images are not re-compressed
  • No watermark, no sign-up, no page limit
  • Flexible ranges — delete single pages and ranges in one go, like 2, 4-6, 9

How to delete pages from a PDF

  • Click "Delete Pages" and drop your file (or click to browse)
  • Type the pages to remove — single pages or ranges, comma-separated
  • Press "Process → Download" and get a new PDF with those pages gone

FAQ

Is deleting PDF pages here really free?

Yes — free and unlimited, with no watermark. Processing happens on your own device, so there is no server cost for us to meter.

Will the remaining pages change in quality?

No. Pages you keep are copied losslessly from the original file — no re-rendering, no re-compression, no quality loss.

Can I delete every page?

No — a PDF must contain at least one page, so the tool refuses to delete all of them. If you need to keep only a few pages, try the Split PDF tool instead.

Can I undo a deletion?

The original file is never modified — the result downloads as a new file, so your original stays exactly as it was.
