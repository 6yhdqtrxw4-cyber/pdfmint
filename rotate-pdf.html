Why rotate a PDF on-device?

Scanned documents are often sensitive: IDs, contracts, bank statements, medical forms. Most online rotators upload your file to a server before turning a single page. PDFMint runs a full PDF engine (pdf-lib) inside your browser tab, so:

  • No upload — the file never leaves your device; you can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads
  • Rotation is saved permanently — the page rotation flag is written into the downloaded PDF, so it displays correctly in every viewer, not just yours
  • No watermark, no sign-up, no page limit
  • Lossless — pages are not re-rendered or re-compressed; text stays selectable and image quality is untouched

How to rotate PDF pages

  • Click "Rotate PDF" and drop your file (or click to browse)
  • Pick the angle: 90° clockwise, 180°, or 270° clockwise (= 90° counter-clockwise)
  • Press "Process → Download" — the rotated PDF saves straight to your device

FAQ

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. The rotation angle is embedded in the PDF file itself, so the document opens rotated in Adobe Reader, Chrome, Preview and every other standards-compliant viewer.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Rotation only changes page metadata — the underlying text and images are copied byte-for-byte without re-compression.

Can I rotate just one page?

The current version rotates all pages at once. A per-page picker is on the roadmap.

My PDF is password-protected. Can I rotate it?

Files that open without a password but restrict editing usually work. Files that require a password to open cannot be processed yet.
